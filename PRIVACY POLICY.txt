PRIVACY POLICY
Last updated: June 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how we, a team of independent developers, collect, use, and protect information when you use our Game. By using the Game, you agree to the terms of data processing described in this document.

1. DATA COLLECTION AND USE
We strive to minimize the collection of personal information. We only collect data necessary for the functionality of the game process:

Identification Data: Your Discord ID, email address (for verification), and nickname.

Technical Data: IP address (for security and abuse prevention), device data, and game activity logs.

User-Generated Content (UGC): Character biographies, clan icons, and other images you upload to the Game.

2. USE OF THIRD-PARTY SERVICES
We rely on third-party infrastructure to operate the Game. Please note that by using the Game, you are also interacting with these services directly:

Google: For sending system emails and registration verification.

Discord: For authorization and operation of the reporting system.

imgBB: For hosting and displaying images uploaded by users.

GitHub: For storing source code, game updates, and syncing security lists.

Boosty: For processing voluntary donations. We do not store your payment information (card numbers, etc.); all transaction processing takes place on Boosty's end.

We are not responsible for the privacy policies of these companies. We recommend that you review their terms on their respective websites.

3. DATA STORAGE AND SECURITY
We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information. However, no method of data transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute protection against hacking or third-party system failures.

4. USER RIGHTS
You have the right to:

Request information about the data we store about you.

Delete your account and associated data from our database (except where such deletion would interfere with system functionality or project security).

Submit a complaint via the in-game system if you believe your data is being misused.

5. CHILDREN
The Game is intended for individuals aged 13 and older (recommended age is 16+). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected such data, we will delete it immediately.

6. CHANGES TO THE POLICY
We reserve the right to modify this Policy at any time. Continued use of the Game after the publication of updates signifies your acceptance of the new version of the Policy.
